## D. Weaknesses

Recent posts get seen but not engaged with: 5 of 10 non-viral posts have 0 likes despite 10,000 to 16,000 views each.

- **Text and photo jokes get nothing.** 3 likes in total.
- **Body-shaming posts flop.** The #fatgirls post and the dating-app weight joke: 11,835 and 15,805 views, 0 likes. They make the page read as a troll account.
- **Captions describe the poster, not the clip.** "I'm high as balls... Enjoy the show": 15,201 views, 0 likes.
- **Hashtag clutter.** 2 to 4 tags per post; some (#goon, #gore) may be treated as adult or graphic.
- **Posting time.** Most recent posts went out 10 PM to 3 AM Eastern; the viral one at 11:02 AM Eastern on a Monday. One example, worth testing.

Risks:

- **The #gore tag.** X requires a content warning on graphic media, and [gratuitous gore](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/violent-speech) is not allowed. If the viral video is removed, the best asset goes with it. Mark it sensitive and drop #gore.
- **Ad revenue.** [X keeps ads away from posts labelled sensitive](https://business.x.com/en/help/ads-policies/brand-safety).
- **Possible reach limits.** Repeated 0-like posts with thousands of views can mean a warning or [restricted reach](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/media-literacy-plan). Suspicion, not a finding.

## E. The viral formula (hypothesis)

1. **Real, unscripted conflict.**
2. **A long payoff.** 53.7 seconds vs 8.6 to 13.5 seconds for other videos.
3. **Something to argue about.** 47 replies, more than every other post combined.
4. **The clip speaks for itself.** Caption was only hashtags.
5. **Daytime posting.** 11:02 AM Eastern, Monday.

Repeatable version: a real, high-stakes clip with a clear "who's wrong?" moment + a short Joker-voice caption that takes a side or asks the question + posted in the daytime + marked sensitive if graphic.
