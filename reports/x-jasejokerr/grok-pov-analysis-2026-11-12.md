### What Didn’t (or Worked Differently)

- Text/photo posts and body-related commentary: Views without engagement. Risk of being perceived as low-effort or troll-ish.
- Late-night posting: Consistently lower engagement in the data.
- Over-reliance on public trackers: SuperX (and similar) miss posts, undercount, or lag. Private Analytics is non-negotiable for decisions.

### Risks & Context
- Adult/twerk content lives in a high-competition, high-bot environment. Aggregators like @SimolMota are common and can help or dilute.
- Sensitive media policies, potential reduced reach, or account actions if content crosses lines (gore tags, explicitness).
- Mixed content strategy dilutes the signal. The algorithm prefers accounts that consistently deliver what users engage with.

1. **Double down on the winner format**: Short (10–30s), high-quality visual adult/twerk clips with 1–3 targeted hashtags, minimal caption. Post when you can monitor early engagement (late morning / afternoon ET).

5. **Clean up weak content**: Reduce or stop zero-engagement text/photo posts. Mark sensitive content appropriately. Consider removing or archiving the highest-risk gore tags if reach is being throttled.
