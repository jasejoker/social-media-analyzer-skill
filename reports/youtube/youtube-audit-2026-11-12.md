# YouTube audit: @jasejoker and @jasejokerr

Collected 2026-11-12 from YouTube Studio (Jordan's own Chrome, signed in). All numbers are from Studio, window "last 28 days" (Oct 15 to Nov 11, 2026) unless stated. Nothing was changed on either channel.

## Start here: the 6 findings

1. **Both channels are tiny and far from monetization.** See the table below. The ad tiers need millions of Shorts views; these channels have tens of thousands.
2. **One or two Shorts did almost everything.** On @jasejoker, two Shorts posted Oct 20 produced 72% of all Shorts views. On @jasejokerr, one Short (Oct 20) produced 18.1K of 23.3K views.
3. **Views almost never become subscribers.** @jasejoker: 14 new subs from 60.5K views (0.023%). @jasejokerr: 8 new subs from 23.3K views (0.034%). Shorts viewers scroll past.
4. **@jasejoker does better than @jasejokerr because of volume and consistency**, not a secret. 12 Shorts since Oct 20 vs 3 on @jasejokerr (which also stopped posting after Oct 31).
5. **Reposted TikTok clips are a monetization risk**, and several already have copyright claims (list below).
6. **2,281 of @jasejokerr's views are labelled "YouTube advertising" in Studio**, which normally means a paid promotion. Jordan said he has no payment method, so this needs checking. Paid views generally don't count toward monetization thresholds.

## Monetization distance (from Studio's Earn page, data as of Nov 7, 2026)

| Requirement | @jasejoker | @jasejokerr | Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | 202 | 404 | 500 (early tier) / 1,000 (ads) |
| Qualified Shorts views, 90 days | 25K | 10K | 3M (early tier) / 10M (ads) |
| Qualified watch hours, 365 days | 25 | 0 | 3,000 (early tier) / 4,000 (ads) |
| Public uploads, last 90 days | 3 required | 3 required | 3 |

- Studio's "qualified" Shorts views (25K) are well below total views (about 56K from Shorts), so a large share of views does not count. Cause not confirmed.
- Shorts watch time does not count toward the 4,000 watch hours.
- Rule change: on Feb 1, 2027 the ad-tier entry bar for new applicants roughly doubles (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views, plus 1,000 subs). Already-partnered channels are grandfathered. Source: YouTube's Aug 2026 announcement, reported by Tubefilter and others.
- At 60K views per 28 days, @jasejoker would collect roughly 150K Shorts views in 90 days. That is about 1.5% of the 10M needed.

## Why @jasejoker beats @jasejokerr

- 4x more Shorts in the same period (12 vs 3). More posts means more chances at a breakout.
- Steady posting: 11 posts between Oct 24 and Nov 10. @jasejokerr posted 3 times and went quiet.
- Both channels had their one big spike on the same two days (Oct 20 to 22), so it was probably luck on the topic or timing. @jasejoker simply had two of those instead of one.
- Neither channel has repeatable winners. After the spike, @jasejoker's median is about 1.3K views.
- Not enough data to say what theme or format wins. The two best Shorts are different topics (satisfying clip, shark-tank-style clip).

## Risks

- **Copyright claims on reposted clips:** "Bro is having a bad day" (blocked in some territories, @jasejoker), "So true #gaming #gta6" (blocked in some territories, @jasejokerr), "Gravity undefeated" (notice). A claim is not a strike, but repeated claims and re-used clips from TikTok can fail YouTube's reused / unoriginal content review at the monetization stage.
- **Paid views on @jasejokerr** (see finding 6): paid views usually don't count.
- **Old trading videos on @jasejoker** (stock call options, "live trading") and drafts are outside the meme theme.
